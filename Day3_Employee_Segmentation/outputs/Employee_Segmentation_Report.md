# Day 3 Deliverable: Employee Segmentation Report

## Executive Summary
Using unsupervised machine learning, **600 employees** were segmented into 4 distinct operational workforce cohorts to enable targeted internal mobility and L&D programs.

## Workforce Segments Breakdown
- **Steady / Stable Performers**: 294 employees (49.0%)
- **Early Career - Actively Upskilling**: 157 employees (26.2%)
- **Skill Gap - Needs Targeted Upskilling**: 149 employees (24.8%)

## Strategic Recommendations by Cohort
1. **High Performers - Mobility Ready**: Fast-track for open internal roles, succession management, and leadership development.
2. **Early Career - Actively Upskilling**: Provide structured mentorship, cross-functional projects, and advanced skill certifications.
3. **Skill Gap - Needs Targeted Upskilling**: Assign mandatory targeted training modules to bridge technical skill deficits.
4. **Steady / Stable Performers**: Maintain role engagement, lateral domain expansions, and peer coaching roles.
